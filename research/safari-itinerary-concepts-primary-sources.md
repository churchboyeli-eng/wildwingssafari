# Safari itinerary concepts from primary sources

Research date: 2026-08-30

Purpose: give Wild Wings seven differentiated safari products for the Safaris page at 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12 and 21 days. The routes below are product concepts for a private operator, not fixed park-authority itineraries. Confirm air schedules, road conditions, permits, accommodation and current park tariffs before publishing a quote.

## How to use these concepts

- Treat each duration as a complete travel window, including arrival, transfers and the final connection. The number of game-drive days is deliberately stated so the selling promise stays honest.
- Use the route arc, selling angle and park fact as the card content. Keep the detailed day plan inside the itinerary page.
- Do not promise a particular migration sighting. Serengeti's movement is seasonal and weather-led; match the route to the client's month and explain that the guide responds to current sightings.
- Show prices only after the operator has confirmed season, accommodation level, group size, park fees, flights and rooming. The official sources below are destination references, not package quotes.

## Seven product concepts

### 1. First Tracks: Tarangire, Crater and Serengeti

- **Duration:** 5 days / 4 nights
- **Route arc:** Arusha → Tarangire → Ngorongoro Conservation Area → Central Serengeti → departure
- **Suggested rhythm:**
  - Day 1: Arrive in Arusha, briefing and an easy first evening.
  - Day 2: Tarangire game drive, with elephants, baobabs and the river as the opening story.
  - Day 3: Travel through the highlands and descend into Ngorongoro Crater for a full game drive.
  - Day 4: Continue into Central Serengeti with an en-route game drive.
  - Day 5: Sunrise game drive, then connect by road or air according to the confirmed plan.
- **Selling angle:** A serious first safari in five days: three distinct landscapes, no filler stop and a clear finale in the Serengeti.
- **Distinctive activities:** Full crater descent, private game drives and a sunrise field session.
- **Best match:** First-time visitors with limited leave who still want the Northern Circuit icons.
- **Source-backed rationale:** TANAPA describes Tarangire as elephant and baobab country, Serengeti as migration and Big Five country, and Ngorongoro Conservation Area Authority (NCAA) lists the crater game drive as its most popular activity. [Tarangire National Park, TANAPA](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/tarangire) · [Serengeti National Park, TANAPA](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/serengeti) · [NCAA tourism activities](https://www.ncaa.go.tz/tourism-activities/)

### 2. Southbound: Mikumi and the Rufiji River

- **Duration:** 6 days / 5 nights
- **Route arc:** Dar es Salaam → Mikumi → Nyerere National Park → Rufiji River → Dar es Salaam or Zanzibar connection
- **Suggested rhythm:**
  - Day 1: Arrive in Dar es Salaam and settle close to the departure point.
  - Days 2-3: Mikumi game drives across the Mkata floodplain, with a slower afternoon for birding.
  - Days 4-5: Nyerere game drive plus a boat safari on the Rufiji or connected lakes, subject to the confirmed programme.
  - Day 6: Final river or wildlife session, then connect onward.
- **Selling angle:** A quieter southern alternative that changes the safari medium from road to river without adding a second northern-circuit repeat.
- **Distinctive activities:** Boat safari, guided walk where authorised, floodplain game drive and sunset birding.
- **Best match:** Zanzibar guests or Dar-based travellers who want bush and water, with less emphasis on the classic northern route.
- **Source-backed rationale:** TANAPA identifies Mikumi's Mkata floodplain, road access from Dar es Salaam, dry-season wildlife concentration and designated walking areas. TANAPA identifies Nyerere's Rufiji channels, wild dogs, boat safaris, walking safaris and dawn or late-afternoon viewing. [Mikumi National Park, TANAPA](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/mikumi) · [Nyerere National Park, TANAPA](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/nyerere) · [Nyerere wildlife and habitats, TANAPA](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/nyerere/wildlife)

### 3. Migration Field Notes

- **Duration:** 7 days / 6 nights
- **Route arc:** Arusha → Central Serengeti → Northern Serengeti or the seasonal southern plains → Ngorongoro → departure
- **Suggested rhythm:**
  - Day 1: Arrival, route briefing and a seasonal wildlife update.
  - Day 2: Enter the Serengeti and begin the first field drive.
  - Days 3-5: Three full Serengeti days, moving only when current sightings and the season justify it.
  - Day 6: Ngorongoro Crater or a highland overnight, depending on the client's route and flight timing.
  - Day 7: Final crater or highland experience, then departure.
- **Selling angle:** A migration itinerary that sells time and field judgement rather than a guaranteed photograph.
- **Distinctive activities:** Long field days, dedicated predator viewing and optional balloon safari where available and pre-booked.
- **Best match:** Wildlife-first travellers who can travel in the likely seasonal window and accept a flexible route.
- **Season note:** TANAPA lists June-October as the dry-season migration window and January-February as a calving period. The page should present these as planning windows, not guarantees.
- **Source-backed rationale:** TANAPA describes the migration as involving more than 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras and predators, with Big Five wildlife and game drives, walking and balloon experiences. [Serengeti National Park, TANAPA](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/serengeti) · [Mara River migration crossings guideline, TANAPA](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1682869509-MARARIVERCROSSINGGUIDELINE.pdf)

### 4. The Northern Canvas

- **Duration:** 8 days / 7 nights
- **Route arc:** Arusha → Tarangire → Lake Manyara → Ngorongoro highlands → Central Serengeti → departure
- **Suggested rhythm:**
  - Day 1: Arrival in Arusha and trip briefing.
  - Day 2: Tarangire game drive and baobab landscapes.
  - Day 3: Lake Manyara game drive, groundwater forest and lake shore.
  - Day 4: Ngorongoro highlands, with an optional authorised walk or cultural or archaeological visit.
  - Day 5: Crater-floor game drive, then overnight near the rim or in Karatu.
  - Days 6-7: Two full Serengeti days in the central or seasonally appropriate area.
  - Day 8: Final drive or flight connection.
- **Selling angle:** The Northern Circuit as a progression of textures: river and baobab, forest and lake, crater and open plain.
- **Distinctive activities:** Lake Manyara canopy walk where available, crater descent, and a choice of archaeology, culture or walking in NCA with the required guide or ranger.
- **Best match:** Travellers who want variety and a more complete first safari without the long duration of a grand circuit.
- **Source-backed rationale:** TANAPA describes Manyara's Rift Valley escarpment, tree-climbing lions, groundwater forest, flamingos and canopy walkway. NCAA lists walking, cultural tours, archaeology, nature walks and birding in the Conservation Area. [Lake Manyara National Park, TANAPA](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/lake-manyara) · [Lake Manyara attractions, TANAPA](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/lake-manyara/attractions) · [NCAA tourism activities](https://www.ncaa.go.tz/tourism-activities/)

### 5. North, South, One Tanzania

- **Duration:** 10 days / 9 nights
- **Route arc:** Arusha → Tarangire → Ngorongoro → Serengeti → fly south → Nyerere → Dar es Salaam or Zanzibar
- **Suggested rhythm:**
  - Days 1-2: Arusha arrival and Tarangire.
  - Day 3: Ngorongoro highlands and crater-floor game drive.
  - Days 4-6: Central or seasonal Serengeti, with enough nights to avoid a drive-through visit.
  - Day 7: Domestic flight and Nyerere arrival.
  - Days 8-9: Nyerere game drive and Rufiji boat safari.
  - Day 10: Morning wildlife session and onward connection.
- **Selling angle:** Two safari personalities in one trip: the northern plains and the southern river country.
- **Distinctive activities:** Serengeti big-game days followed by a boat safari and, where authorised, a guided walk in Nyerere.
- **Best match:** Repeat Tanzania visitors, photographers and clients who want the famous parks plus a less common southern chapter.
- **Logistics note:** This should be sold as a fly-between-regions itinerary. Do not imply it is a continuous road transfer unless a separate long-drive version is quoted.
- **Source-backed rationale:** The official Tanzania Tourism Board promotes the Tarangire-Ngorongoro-Serengeti northern circuit. TANAPA gives Nyerere a distinct river, floodplain and woodland identity and lists boat and walking safaris. [Northern Tanzania safari circuit, Tanzania Tourist Board](https://site.tanzaniatourism.go.tz/page/tarangire-ngorongoro-and-serengeti-) · [Nyerere National Park, TANAPA](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/nyerere)

### 6. Southern Wilderness: Mikumi, Nyerere and Ruaha

- **Duration:** 12 days / 11 nights
- **Route arc:** Dar es Salaam → Mikumi → Nyerere → Ruaha → Dar es Salaam or onward connection
- **Suggested rhythm:**
  - Day 1: Dar es Salaam arrival and road or flight preparation.
  - Days 2-3: Mikumi, with Mkata floodplain drives and an unhurried birding window.
  - Days 4-6: Nyerere, combining game drives, Rufiji boat safari and a walking or fly-camp option only when authorised.
  - Days 7-10: Ruaha, with several dawn and late-afternoon drives around the Great Ruaha and Mwagusi river systems.
  - Day 11: Final Ruaha drive and flight or road connection.
  - Day 12: Departure or optional Zanzibar extension.
- **Selling angle:** A deep southern safari for travellers who value space, rivers, unusual antelope and time away from the standard Northern Circuit.
- **Distinctive activities:** Rufiji boat safari, Ruaha walking safari or stargazing, plus specialist searches for wild dogs, kudu and sable.
- **Best match:** Repeat safari travellers, photographers and clients who prefer wilderness character over a checklist of famous stops.
- **Source-backed rationale:** TANAPA describes Mikumi as a southern gateway with floodplain viewing, Nyerere as a Rufiji and wild-dog stronghold, and Ruaha as a 20,226 km² wilderness with greater kudu, the Great Ruaha River, walking, game drives and stargazing. [Mikumi National Park, TANAPA](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/mikumi) · [Nyerere National Park, TANAPA](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/nyerere) · [Ruaha National Park, TANAPA](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/ruaha) · [Ruaha wildlife and habitats, TANAPA](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/ruaha/wildlife)

### 7. The Grand Tanzania Route

- **Duration:** 21 days / 20 nights
- **Route arc:** Arusha → Tarangire → Lake Manyara → Ngorongoro → Serengeti → fly south → Nyerere → Mikumi or Ruaha → Zanzibar extension → departure
- **Suggested rhythm:**
  - Days 1-2: Arrive and settle in Arusha, with Tarangire as the opening park.
  - Days 3-4: Lake Manyara and the Ngorongoro highlands, allowing time for a crater, culture or archaeology choice.
  - Days 5-9: Five Serengeti nights or a seasonal split between southern, central and northern areas.
  - Days 10-13: Fly south for Nyerere, with game drives and a Rufiji boat safari.
  - Days 14-16: Mikumi or Ruaha, selected around the client's interest in access, river country, wild dogs, kudu or birding.
  - Days 17-20: Zanzibar recovery chapter, with Stone Town, spice, marine or beach days chosen by the client.
  - Day 21: Departure.
- **Selling angle:** One country, several rhythms: highland geology, open plains, southern rivers and Swahili coast. It is a private travel story rather than a long list of parks.
- **Distinctive activities:** Crater descent, an NCA culture or archaeology option, migration-led Serengeti days, Rufiji boat safari, a southern specialist park and a flexible Zanzibar finish.
- **Best match:** Long-haul travellers who want a once-in-a-lifetime Tanzania journey, or guests adding a substantial coast finish after the safari.
- **Planning note:** Offer this as a modular route with two or three comfort levels. The client should be able to shorten the south, extend Serengeti or choose Zanzibar without rewriting the whole proposal.
- **Source-backed rationale:** NCAA gives the northern highlands a combined wildlife, human-history, archaeological and cultural identity. TANAPA documents the northern and southern parks as distinct experiences. The Tanzania Tourist Board describes Zanzibar experiences across heritage, beach, marine, spice and nature themes. [NCAA tourism activities](https://www.ncaa.go.tz/tourism-activities/) · [TANAPA national parks](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/tourism) · [Tanzania Tourist Board](https://www.tanzaniatourism.go.tz/) · [Zanzibar Commission for Tourism](https://www.zanzibartourism.go.tz/)

## Park fact bank for itinerary detail pages

Use these facts to expand the itinerary pages without turning the cards into encyclopaedia copy:

- **Serengeti:** TANAPA describes more than 1.5 million migrating wildebeest, zebras and predators, Big Five wildlife, 500-plus bird species, balloon safaris and game drives. It lists June-October for migration viewing and January-February for calving. [TANAPA Serengeti](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/serengeti)
- **Tarangire:** TANAPA highlights the Tarangire River, large elephant concentrations, ancient baobabs, night drives, bush meals and walking with an armed guide. The official visitor guide says the dry season is June-October and recommends an authorised guide for walks. [TANAPA Tarangire](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/tarangire) · [Tarangire visitor guide](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/tarangire/visitor-guide)
- **Lake Manyara:** TANAPA highlights the Rift Valley escarpment, tree-climbing lions, groundwater forest, flamingos, canopy walks, birding and hippo pools. [TANAPA Lake Manyara](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/lake-manyara) · [Lake Manyara wildlife and habitats](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/lake-manyara/wildlife)
- **Ngorongoro:** NCAA presents the crater as a World Heritage Site and Man and Biosphere Reserve, and lists crater drives, walking, cultural tours with Maasai, Datoga and Hadzabe communities, archaeology at Olduvai and Laetoli, nature walks and birding. NCAA says NCA can be visited year-round, while walking activities require prior arrangement and an armed ranger. [NCAA tourism activities](https://www.ncaa.go.tz/tourism-activities/) · [NCAA home](https://www.ncaa.go.tz/)
- **Mikumi:** TANAPA describes Mkata floodplain game drives, elephants, lions, buffalo, zebra, giraffe, hippo and birding. It lists June-October as the dry-season viewing window and notes that green season can be strong for birds. [TANAPA Mikumi](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/mikumi)
- **Nyerere:** TANAPA highlights the Rufiji River, oxbow lakes, hippos, crocodiles, elephants, wild dogs, boat safaris, walking safaris and game drives. It describes June-November as dry season and December-February as a green period when many areas remain accessible. [TANAPA Nyerere](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/nyerere) · [Nyerere brochure](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1717683069-Nyerere%20brochure%20-Eng%202024.pdf)
- **Ruaha:** TANAPA highlights the Great Ruaha River, greater and lesser kudu, large buffalo herds, elephants, predators, 570-plus bird species, walking safaris, game drives and stargazing. [TANAPA Ruaha](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/ruaha) · [Ruaha wildlife and habitats](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/ruaha/wildlife)
- **Zanzibar extension:** The Tanzania Tourist Board's official portal frames Tanzania around wildlife, culture, beaches, marine experiences and food. Keep Zanzibar as a deliberate recovery or culture chapter rather than an unexplained add-on. [Tanzania Tourist Board](https://www.tanzaniatourism.go.tz/) · [Zanzibar Commission for Tourism](https://www.zanzibartourism.go.tz/)

## Content and booking guardrails

- Label the cards **5 days**, **6 days**, **7 days**, **8 days**, **10 days**, **12 days** and **21 days**. Do not hide the travel days inside vague labels such as “one week”.
- Pair every card with a one-line reason to choose it: first safari, river safari, migration focus, full Northern Circuit, north plus south, southern wilderness or grand Tanzania.
- For seasonal products, show the planning window and a short disclaimer next to the price. This prevents the client reading “migration” as a guaranteed river crossing.
- Keep activities conditional where an authority requires it: “where authorised”, “with prior arrangement” or “subject to the confirmed lodge programme”.
- Link each detailed page to the relevant official park or NCAA reference in an “Explore the places” section. That is a useful Wild Wings differentiator: clients can verify the place and understand why the route is shaped this way.
