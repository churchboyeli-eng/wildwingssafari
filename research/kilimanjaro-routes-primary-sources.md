# Kilimanjaro route research from primary sources

Research date: 2026-08-30

Purpose: give Wild Wings accurate, useful route material for a Kilimanjaro itineraries page. The route cards below are product-planning briefs, not park-authority packages or promises of a summit. Distances and days can change with the selected gate, route variant, camp allocation, weather and operator. Confirm current park access, permits, tariffs, camp availability and any route restrictions with TANAPA/KINAPA before quoting.

## What the official record calls a route

TANAPA's current Kilimanjaro material is not completely consistent about the count. Its 2024 Kilimanjaro National Park map labels Marangu, Machame, Umbwe, Lemosho, Shira, Rongai, Mweka and Northern Circuit. The map also marks camps and landmarks including Mandara, Horombo, Kibo, Shira 1, Shira 2, Moir, School Hut, Barafu, Barranco, Karanga, Mweka and Uhuru Peak. [TANAPA GIS map, 2024](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1730810409-QUICKREFERENCEGUIDEFINAL.pdf)

TANAPA's park special edition describes six ascending routes as Marangu, Machame, Umbwe, Lemosho, Londorosi and Rongai, with Mweka used for descent by climbers on the western and southern routes. Its older visitor brochure says there are at least seven trails and that a summit climb takes five to nine days or longer. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro special edition](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1678818900-KNPMGZNE.pdf) · [TANAPA Kilimanjaro brochure](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1581671752-TANAPA%20GENERAL%20BROCHURES%202020-WEBSITE%20%281%29.pdf)

For a client-facing site, use seven route stories: Marangu, Machame, Lemosho, Rongai, Northern Circuit, Umbwe and Shira. Explain the naming clearly:

- **Lemosho** normally enters through Londorossi. **Shira** is the older high-start western variant and overlaps Lemosho after the Shira Plateau; many operators now sell Lemosho instead because its lower start is easier to manage for acclimatisation.
- **Northern Circuit** uses the western/Lemosho approach in the common itinerary, then traverses the quiet northern side before returning to the summit route.
- **Mweka** is a descent trail, not a customer-facing summit route.
- **Kilema** is shown by TANAPA as a cycling route, including a summit-bound option, rather than one of the standard trekking products below. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro activities and facilities](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1633027058-KI.pdf) · [TANAPA Kilimanjaro park page](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/kilimanjaro)

TANAPA says the more days spent climbing, the higher the possibility of reaching the summit, and its brochure describes the mountain as a five-to-nine-day-or-longer climb. The current TANAPA visitor guide also says all climbs must be guided by a licensed operator and that fees depend on route and number of days. ## Route briefs for Wild Wings

### 1. Marangu - the hut route

- **Start:** Marangu Gate on the south-eastern side. Marangu is also the park headquarters; TANAPA places it 41 km from Moshi and 86 km from Kilimanjaro International Airport. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro brochure](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1633027058-KI.pdf)
- **Standard arc:** Marangu Gate → Mandara Hut → Horombo Hut → Kibo Hut → Gilman's Point / crater rim → Uhuru Peak → Horombo → Marangu Gate.
- **Key places to name:** Mandara and Horombo huts, Maundi Crater, Kibo Hut, Mawenzi views and the summit approach. TANAPA says Maundi Crater is about 15 minutes beyond Mandara huts and gives views toward Lakes Chala, Jipe and Nyumba ya Mungu. Kifinika is a sacred Chagga cultural site along the Marangu route. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro attractions](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1633027058-KI.pdf)
- **Sleeping:** The only standard ascent with permanent hut accommodation at Mandara, Horombo and Kibo; other wilderness routes use designated camps. [TANAPA facilities](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1633027058-KI.pdf)
- **Selling point:** the most straightforward underfoot and the most comfortable in wet weather, with a gentler gradient and huts. Be honest that ascending and descending on the same trail reduces scenery variety and the 5-day plan compresses acclimatisation.
- **Wild Wings card line:** "A warm hut each night, a slower 6-day rhythm and sunrise over Kibo."
- **Best fit:** guests who prioritise huts, a known route and a six-day option; not guests choosing only because the 5-day label looks cheaper.

Altezza's route page confirms the 5- and 6-day variations, the Marangu Gate-to-Mandara start, the hut accommodation, and the operator's recommendation of the longer variation for better acclimatisation. Treat the operator's summit-success figures as that operator's own dataset, not a universal promise. [Altezza Marangu route guide](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/marangu)

### 2. Machame - the scenic southern traverse

- **Start:** Machame Gate on the south-western side. The opening day passes through rainforest before reaching Machame Camp. [Altezza Machame route guide](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/machame)
- **Standard arc:** Machame Gate → Machame Camp → Shira Camp → Lava Tower → Barranco Camp → Karanga Camp on the 7-day version → Barafu Camp → Uhuru Peak → Millennium/Mweka Camp → Mweka Gate.
- **Key places to name:** the rainforest, Shira Plateau, Lava Tower, Barranco Wall, Karanga Valley, Barafu and the southern summit approach. TANAPA's map confirms the camps and landmarks; the operator itinerary documents the Shira-to-Lava-Tower-to-Barranco sequence and the Mweka descent. [TANAPA GIS map](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1730810409-QUICKREFERENCEGUIDEFINAL.pdf) · [Altezza Machame itinerary](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/machame)
- **Technical note:** The Barranco Wall is a steep scramble in the normal route description, not a technical rock-climbing pitch. State that scrambling and exposed terrain require a steady pace and guide instruction; never call it "easy" for every client.
- **Acclimatisation:** the 7-day itinerary adds Karanga and is the better default. Altezza describes 6-day Machame as suitable only for experienced trekkers or travellers with prior acclimatisation.
- **Selling point:** the strongest visual progression from forest to moorland, alpine desert and summit, with a different descent trail.
- **Wild Wings card line:** "Five climate zones in one south-west traverse, with Barranco Wall and a fresh forest descent."
- **Best fit:** fit first-time climbers who want scenery and can commit to seven days; avoid selling 6 days as the normal beginner option.

### 3. Lemosho - the western approach

- **Start variants:** The full 8-day option enters at Londorossi Gate and walks from the lower western forest. Altezza's 6- and 7-day products use a vehicle drop-off on the Shira Plateau, around 3,414-3,600 m. This distinction matters for both logistics and altitude exposure. [Altezza Lemosho route guide](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/lemosho)
- **Standard arc:** Londorossi / western drop-off → Mti Mkubwa or Shira 1 → Shira 2 → Cathedral Point or acclimatisation hike → Lava Tower → Barranco → Karanga → Barafu → Uhuru Peak → Mweka Camp → Mweka Gate.
- **Key places to name:** Shira 1 and 2, Shira Cathedral/Cathedral Point, Lava Tower, Barranco Wall, Karanga, Barafu and the Mweka rainforest descent. The operator's itinerary identifies the Shira camps, Cathedral Point acclimatisation option, Lava Tower and Barranco sequence. [Altezza Lemosho itinerary](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/lemosho)
- **Acclimatisation:** the route earns its premium through a longer, more gradual western approach. The 8-day lower-start itinerary is the cleanest default for a guest without recent altitude experience; the 6-day high-drop version should not be presented as equivalent.
- **Selling point:** remote-feeling opening days, Shira Plateau views, giant groundsels and a strong "arrive slowly" story.
- **Wild Wings card line:** "The west-side opening: quiet forest, Shira Plateau and more time to settle into the mountain."
- **Best fit:** first-time climbers who want the safest-feeling pace and guests who value landscape variety over the lowest price.

### 4. Rongai - the northern approach

- **Start:** Nalemuru/Rongai Gate on the northern slope near the Kenya border. Altezza's itinerary starts at Nalemuru Gate at about 2,020 m and finishes through Marangu Gate. [Altezza Rongai route guide](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/rongai)
- **Standard arc:** Nalemuru Gate → Simba Camp → Second Cave / Kikilewa Camp → Mawenzi Tarn → Kibo Hut → Uhuru Peak → Horombo Hut → Marangu Gate.
- **Key places to name:** coniferous forest, Simba Camp, Kikilewa, Mawenzi Tarn at the foot of Mawenzi, Kibo Hut, the Kibo crater rim and the Marangu descent. The operator itinerary gives the Simba, Kikilewa, Mawenzi Tarn, Kibo, Horombo and Marangu sequence; TANAPA's map labels Simba, Second Cave, Kikilewa, Kibo and Horombo. [Altezza Rongai itinerary](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/rongai) · [TANAPA GIS map](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1730810409-QUICKREFERENCEGUIDEFINAL.pdf)
- **Character:** a quieter, drier northern slope with less dense vegetation on the way up and a different ecosystem on the Marangu descent. Do not promise solitude or wildlife sightings; use "usually quieter" and "drier approach".
- **Acclimatisation:** the 7-day itinerary is the better commercial default. The summit push and descent to Horombo can be a 12-14-hour day in the referenced itinerary, so the card should call out endurance rather than imply an easy route.
- **Selling point:** see both sides of Kilimanjaro in one climb: quiet north on ascent, forested south on descent.
- **Wild Wings card line:** "From the dry northern forest to Mawenzi Tarn, then down through Marangu."
- **Best fit:** repeat climbers, guests who want a less crowded start and travellers considering a wet-season alternative; still require normal altitude screening.

### 5. Northern Circuit - the long way around Kibo

- **Start:** commonly Londorossi Gate with a western/Shira Plateau drop-off; some operators describe a northern-side variant. Use the exact start in the client's quote. [Altezza Northern Circuit route guide](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/northern-circuit)
- **Standard arc:** Londorossi / Shira 1 → Shira 2 → Lava Tower → Moir Hut → Pofu/Buffalo Camp → 3rd Cave → School Hut → Uhuru Peak → Mweka Camp → Mweka Gate.
- **Key places to name:** Shira Plateau, Lava Tower, Moir Valley, northern slopes, Buffalo Camp, 3rd Cave, School Hut, the Kibo summit route and Mweka descent. Altezza's day-by-day plan gives Shira 1/2, Moir Hut, Buffalo Camp, 3rd Cave and School Hut, while TANAPA's map confirms the route and facilities. [Altezza Northern Circuit itinerary](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/northern-circuit) · [TANAPA GIS map](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1730810409-QUICKREFERENCEGUIDEFINAL.pdf)
- **Acclimatisation:** the additional nights and gradual northern traverse give the strongest acclimatisation profile in the comparison, but the route is long. The main risk to explain is accumulated fatigue, not a short "easy route" label.
- **Selling point:** the most complete mountain story, with long stretches away from the busier southern trails and views over the Kenya side.
- **Wild Wings card line:** "A full arc around Kibo for guests who want time, silence and the widest view of the mountain."
- **Best fit:** guests with nine or more available days, strong walking habits and a preference for low traffic over the shortest quote.

### 6. Umbwe - the direct ridge

- **Start:** Umbwe Gate on the southern side, around 1,800 m in the operator's route description. [Altezza Umbwe route guide](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/umbwe)
- **Standard arc:** Umbwe Gate → Umbwe Camp → Barranco Camp → Barranco Wall → Karanga → Barafu → Uhuru Peak → Millennium/Mweka Camp → Mweka Gate.
- **Key places to name:** rainforest ridge, Umbwe Camp, Barranco Camp, Barranco Wall, Karanga, Barafu and the Mweka descent. The route joins the Machame/Lemosho line around Barranco; TANAPA's map shows Umbwe, Barranco, Karanga, Barafu and Mweka. [Altezza Umbwe itinerary](https://altezzatravel.com/climbing-kilimanjaro/routes/umbwe) · [TANAPA GIS map](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1730810409-QUICKREFERENCEGUIDEFINAL.pdf)
- **Difficulty:** steep and direct at the start, with a significant first-day altitude gain. The first two days are the selling reality. It is not a technical climb in the standard summit itinerary, but it demands confident footing and conditioning.
- **Acclimatisation:** lead with a 7-day product. The 6-day version is for experienced high-altitude trekkers or clients with a clear acclimatisation history; it should not be positioned as a budget shortcut for beginners.
- **Selling point:** quiet opening days and a dramatic ridge before joining the classic southern traverse.
- **Wild Wings card line:** "The bold opening: a quiet rainforest ridge that meets the mountain's famous southern trail at Barranco."
- **Best fit:** experienced hikers who want a direct, less crowded start and understand that short does not mean easy.

### 7. Shira Plateau - the high-start variant

- **Start:** Londorossi/Shira access road, with the hiking section at the Shira Barrier around 3,595 m. The older route is also called Shira Route or, in some descriptions, Londorossi Route. [Altezza Shira Plateau research](https://altezzatravel.com/articles/shira-of-kilimanjaro)
- **Standard arc:** Shira Barrier → Simba Camp → Shira 2 → Lava Tower → Barranco → Karanga → Barafu → Uhuru Peak → Mweka Gate.
- **Key places to name:** the collapsed Shira caldera, Shira Plateau, Simba Camp, Shira 2, Lava Tower, Barranco, Karanga and Barafu. TANAPA identifies Shira as the oldest collapsed volcanic cone and describes the Plateau as a scenic habitat used by large mammals; its map labels Shira, Simba, Shira 1/2, Lava Tower and the southern camps. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro brochure](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1633027058-KI.pdf) · [TANAPA GIS map](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1730810409-QUICKREFERENCEGUIDEFINAL.pdf)
- **Character:** open grassy and rocky volcanic terrain with strong views, but a high first night. Altezza describes the route as only about 56 km and nearly identical to Lemosho after the opening section.
- **Duration source:** Ultimate Kilimanjaro publishes a seven-day Shira itinerary, with a 56 km route profile and a summit push from Barafu. Use this as an operator planning reference, not as a TANAPA tariff or guarantee. [Ultimate Kilimanjaro Shira route](https://www.ultimatekilimanjaro.com/shira-route/)
- **Acclimatisation:** the high drop-off is the central guardrail. Do not market this as "shorter and easier"; make prior altitude experience, medical screening and a slower variant part of the sales conversation.
- **Selling point:** the oldest volcanic landscape on the mountain and a fast arrival into the high alpine story.
- **Wild Wings card line:** "Walk across the ancient Shira caldera, then join the Lava Tower and Barranco line."
- **Best fit:** experienced or pre-acclimatised trekkers who specifically want the plateau; default first-time clients to an 8-day Lemosho instead.

## Shared landmarks and the story of the mountain

Use these details in the route comparison and detail pages so the route names feel meaningful rather than interchangeable:

- **Uhuru Peak / Kibo:** TANAPA places Kibo at 5,895 m, the highest of Kilimanjaro's three volcanic cones. Mawenzi is 5,149 m and Shira is the older collapsed cone at 3,962 m. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro history](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/kilimanjaro/about)
- **Five ecological zones:** TANAPA describes the climb as a change from montane forest to heath and moorland, alpine desert and the arctic summit zone. This is a more useful selling story than a generic "scenic climb". [TANAPA Kilimanjaro brochure](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1633027058-KI.pdf)
- **Western and southern camps:** TANAPA's official map records Shira 1/2, Moir, Lava Tower, Barranco, Karanga, Barafu, School Hut, Kibo, Horombo, Mandara, Mweka, Simba, Second Cave, Third Cave and Mawenzi Tarn. Use those names consistently in timelines and avoid inventing camp names. [TANAPA GIS map](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1730810409-QUICKREFERENCEGUIDEFINAL.pdf)
- **Non-summit options:** TANAPA lists guided day hikes on Marangu/Shira, nature walks in the lower forest, scenic viewpoints and picnics, crater camping and Mawenzi technical climbing. These should be separate products or add-ons, not mixed into the seven standard summit cards. [TANAPA attractions](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/kilimanjaro/attractions)

## Season, access and safety guardrails

- **Season:** TANAPA describes short rains around October-November, the wetter season around March-May, and generally clearer climbing conditions from late June-September and December-early March. Weather still changes quickly by elevation; quote a planning window, not a weather guarantee. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro brochure](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1633027058-KI.pdf)
- **Guiding:** TANAPA's visitor guide says all climbs must be guided by a licensed operator and solo trekking is not permitted. The park brochure recommends a reputable registered local operator with trained guides who know current ground conditions. [TANAPA visitor guide](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/kilimanjaro/visitor-guide) · [TANAPA Kilimanjaro brochure](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1581671752-TANAPA%20GENERAL%20BROCHURES%202020-WEBSITE%20%281%29.pdf)
- **Altitude:** Make "slow pace, acclimatisation days, hydration and symptom reporting" a standard block on every itinerary page. TANAPA says longer itineraries improve the possibility of reaching Kibo; the operator route pages document acclimatisation hikes and advise climbers to report symptoms to the guide. Do not publish success-rate guarantees. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/kilimanjaro) · [Altezza route duration guide](https://altezzatravel.com/articles/how-long-to-climb-kilimanjaro)
- **Camps and rescue:** TANAPA says professional designated camps with toilets are available on the wilderness routes and that first aid and radio communication are available at all mountain stations. Keep the site's promise to "support" factual and avoid implying a helicopter evacuation is included unless it is explicitly contracted. [TANAPA Kilimanjaro facilities](https://tanzaniaparks.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1633027058-KI.pdf)
- **Tariffs:** Park fees vary by visitor category, route and number of days; conservation, camping or hut and rescue fees may apply. Do not hard-code a route price from a competitor or an old tariff. Show "from" pricing only after Wild Wings has confirmed party size, season, accommodation, park fees and crew costs. [TANAPA tariffs](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/tourism/visitor-information/tariff)
- **Western Breach:** It is a high-risk route variant, not one of the seven standard customer-facing cards. Altezza's current route-duration article says the KINAPA temporarily closed it because of rockfall risk. Because this status can change, confirm directly with KINAPA before mentioning it in a quote. [Altezza route-duration guide](https://altezzatravel.com/articles/how-long-to-climb-kilimanjaro)

## Recommended page structure for Wild Wings

1. **Route chooser:** seven cards with duration, start side, sleeping style, difficulty label, "best for" line and an honest acclimatisation note. Keep Mweka and Kilema out of the main list or label them clearly as descent/cycling options.
2. **Route detail:** a route arc at the top, then a day-by-day timeline using the official camp names above. Put gate, approximate distance, high point, sleeping style and the longer-option recommendation in a compact facts row.
3. **Price panel:** show the quote as "from" and keep season, party size, accommodation tier and what is included visible. Add a direct booking or enquiry action beside the price rather than burying it after the route text.
4. **Decision help:** finish with a small "Which route fits?" matrix: huts = Marangu; classic scenery = Machame; best slow build = Lemosho; quiet north = Rongai; full circuit = Northern Circuit; direct ridge = Umbwe; high-start Shira landscape = Shira.
5. **Trust block:** link to TANAPA's Kilimanjaro guide, current tariffs and the relevant official map. This gives the client something verifiable and differentiates Wild Wings from operators that list only route names and a price.
